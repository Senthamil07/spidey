CHAPTER 01 · CURRENT ROLE

The ERP Mission

Meena Fibre Industry Private Limited
March 2026 – Present

"When a business process gets tangled like a web, my job is to help untangle it."

At Meena Fibre Industry, I work as an ERP Functional Consultant. My mission starts with listening. I gather requirements from the people who actually use the processes, understand their daily challenges, and map how their operations should work inside the ERP system.

I'm involved across seven operational areas. Each area has its own process, needs, and challenges. I help translate those business needs into functional workflows that the development team can understand.

Production Inserts Painting Assembly Dispatch Store Tender

Here's where teamwork matters: I act as a bridge between business stakeholders and developers. I also create UI concepts and functional prototypes using Google AI Studio, helping teams explore how a solution could work before moving forward with development.

Once a feature is ready, the mission isn't over. I support functional testing and UAT, identify bugs and workflow gaps, coordinate feedback, validate functionality, and manage change requests. The goal is to help the ERP behave the way the business needs it to.

  • Gather and clarify business requirements.
  • Map operational processes into ERP workflows.
  • Coordinate with developers and stakeholders.
  • Create functional prototypes and UI concepts.
  • Support testing, UAT, bug reporting, and validation.
  • Support change management and process improvement.

"A good solution isn't just one that works on screen. It's one that works for the people using it."
